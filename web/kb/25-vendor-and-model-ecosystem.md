# 25. Vendor and Model Ecosystem

The actual organizations and product families in the market. Children are **providers** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Mistral, DeepSeek, Cohere, xAI, Perplexity) plus the **local/open ecosystem** (LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp, vLLM, Hugging Face). The organizing axis is *who ships the models and on what terms* — closed/frontier vs open-weight/local. This is the concrete, fast-moving layer that instantiates the abstract categories above; expect heavy churn here.
